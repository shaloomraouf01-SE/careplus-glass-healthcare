How this CarePlus demonstration works.

A concept you can explore

CarePlus is a fictional healthcare website concept. Doctor names, experience, ratings, images and contact details are illustrative and do not represent verified providers.

Your form information

Appointment and contact forms generate a preview in your browser. They do not send, save or book anything. Please use fictional details and avoid entering sensitive information.

External resources

Images load from Unsplash and fonts load from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts these providers and may share connection information such as your IP address. Hosting providers may also maintain standard access logs.

Before a real launch

Actual clinic details, provider consent, fees, availability and a secure booking backend must be configured before accepting real patient requests.
