More questions? Get in touch ↗
How do I choose a specialist?

Browse the departments or filter the doctor directory by specialty. Each sample profile links directly to an appointment preview.

Can I book a real appointment here?

This is a demonstration website. You can explore the complete booking flow, but no appointment is sent, reserved or confirmed.

Can I explore online consultations?

Yes. Select video consultation in the appointment form to preview that visit type. No actual video appointment is scheduled.

What should I prepare for a visit?

For an actual visit, ask your chosen clinic about documents, previous reports, fees and any preparation requirements.

Is my form information saved?

No. This demo keeps your input only in the current page. Reloading clears it. Please use sample details when trying the forms.

Where can I find fees and availability?

Live schedules, consultation fees and insurance coverage are not connected to this concept. Confirm these directly with your actual provider.
