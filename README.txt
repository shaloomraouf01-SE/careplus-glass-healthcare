CAREPLUS MEDICAL — MULTI-PAGE HEALTHCARE UI

Open index.html in your browser. No installation or build tools needed.

Pages:
- index.html — Home
- departments.html — Departments
- services.html — Services
- doctors.html — Doctor discovery/search/filter
- appointments.html — Appointment form demo
- contact.html — Contact form demo

Technology: HTML, CSS, Vanilla JavaScript.
All appointment and contact flows are frontend-only demos. No backend, database, payments, real-time doctor slots, authentication or messages.
Images and Google Fonts are loaded from external hosts and require internet. Names, ratings, specialties and contact information are sample placeholder content, not verified doctors or actual practice details.
Change brand name, colors, doctors, images, etc. in the relevant HTML/CSS files.
